# 🗓 First 30 Days — Work Style

Same rule as the rest of the vault: don't do it all on day one. Each week adds one habit.

## Week 1 — log only
Goal: the reflex of stamping and switching.

- [ ] Run the setup ([[08_Meta/Work-Style/Setup|Setup]]) and try the demo once
- [ ] Every workday: log in (`Alt+Shift+I`), log out (`Alt+Shift+O`)
- [ ] Switch activity (`Alt+Shift+S`) at least when you start meetings, breaks and lunch
- [ ] Do not fix anything. Just look at the Today card on Work Home at the end of the day

**Success = 5 logged days, even if the log is rough.**

## Week 2 — plan and prove
- [ ] Give every Top 3 outcome a type and an estimate in minutes
- [ ] Tick tasks off with a `proof` link and an `actual` (or a shared ticket id)
- [ ] Log every blocked and interruption block with who or what caused it
- [ ] Add your first two win lines

**Success = Proof coverage above 60% on Work Home.**

## Week 3 — review
- [ ] Do your first weekly review and read the Work review section top to bottom
- [ ] Pick one experiment from your biggest leak
- [ ] Tag repeated tasks with a `repeat` field; write your first runbook when it asks
- [ ] Add a `skill` property to two Permanent notes and write a teach-back for one

**Success = one experiment chosen from data, not from a feeling.**

## Week 4 — report
- [ ] Do the monthly review; apply one estimate correction
- [ ] Open the [[01_Journal/Reports/Weekly Status Report|Weekly Status Report]] and send it to your manager
- [ ] Export the [[01_Journal/Reports/Timesheet and Attendance Report|Timesheet and Attendance Report]] once, so you know it works
- [ ] Check Work Home's trend table: is deep work up, are leaks down?

**Success = one report you were glad to send.**

## Rules for staying alive past day 30
1. **Lower the bar on bad days.** Login, logout and one switch keeps the record honest.
2. **Never tidy instead of logging.** The Log hygiene list is your only cleanup.
3. **Change one thing per week.** The point of the numbers is one decision, not twenty.
4. **If a field annoys you, drop it.** Only start, cat, est and proof matter on day one.
