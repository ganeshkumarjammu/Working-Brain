# 💼 Work Style — start here

The second brain turns what you *encounter* into what you can *use*. The Work Style layer turns what you *do at work* into what you can *prove*.

Both run on the same vault, the same daily note and the same review loop. You do not need a second system.

---

## The 4 verbs (this is the whole layer)

| Verb | Where | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Log** | today's Daily Note → 💼 Work log | Record login, logout and each activity as it happens. Two seconds per switch. |
| **Prove** | tasks, wins, `[proof:: ...]` fields | Every finished task and win points to a ticket, PR, message or document. |
| **Review** | Weekly · Monthly · Quarterly notes, [[08_Meta/Dashboards/Work Home\|Work Home]] | The numbers write themselves. You read them and decide one change. |
| **Improve** | runbooks, estimate corrections, skills, decisions | Turn every leak and every repeat into a fix you can point to later. |

If you only ever do **Log → Review**, you will still know exactly where your time goes. Proof and improvement are what make it convincing.

Folders say where it lives. Tags say what it is. **Fields in the time log say what happened.**

## Daily loop (adds about 5 minutes to the 10 you already spend)

1. Open today's note (`Alt+Shift+D`), then `Alt+Shift+I` to stamp your login time.
2. Your **Top 3 outcomes** are your plan. Give each an estimate and a type:
   `- [ ] Fix export bug [type:: bugfix] [est:: 90] [ticket:: PRJ-12]`
3. All day: `Alt+Shift+S` when you change activity. Pick the category, type a few words.
4. Tick a task off and add `[proof:: PR 482]`. If it was a win, add a win line.
5. Leave: `Alt+Shift+O`. Two-minute reflection as usual.

## Weekly loop (adds 15 minutes to the 30)

Open the weekly note (`Alt+Shift+W`). Read the Work review, fix anything under "Log hygiene", pick **one** experiment from the biggest leak, then copy the manager update and send it.

## Monthly and quarterly

Monthly: apply the estimate corrections, move skill levels, write the runbooks you were told to write. Quarterly: read the trend, check decision reviews, export the [[01_Journal/Reports/Quarterly Evidence Pack|Quarterly Evidence Pack]].

---

## What keeps the record credible

- **Log during the day.** Same-day timestamps carry more weight than a reconstruction on Friday.
- **Match the company timesheet.** This is the detailed backup to it.
- **Point, don't paste.** Log links to tickets, PRs and documents. Never copy company code, customer data or internal documents into the vault, and check your employment agreement before keeping a personal log.
- **Keep the repo private.** This vault now contains evidence about your job.
- **Honest categories.** Leaks caused by others (blocked, interrupted) are shown separately from leaks you can fix (rework, drift, unlogged). The second list is your improvement plan.
