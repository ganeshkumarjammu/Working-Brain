## 🧮 Money principles worth internalizing
- Savings rate matters more than investment returns, early on
- Lifestyle inflation is the silent killer of wealth
- Time in the market > timing the market
- Never invest in something you can't explain in two sentences
- Insurance is for catastrophes, not inconveniences

## 🪞 Review prompts
- What did I buy this month that I don't remember?
- What subscription am I paying for out of inertia?
- If income dropped 40% tomorrow, how long could I last?
- Am I optimizing pennies while ignoring the big three (housing, transport, income)?
