## ⏱ Focus principles
- One priority at a time. "Priorities" plural is how nothing ships.
- Timebox, don't task-list. Work expands to fill available time.
- The first 90 minutes of the day are worth the next six. Don't sell them to email.
- If it takes <2 minutes, do it now. Otherwise capture it.
- Say no by default. Every yes is a mortgage on future time.

## 🪞 Review prompts
- What did I do this week that a template, script, or someone else could have done?
- Where did my time actually go vs where I intended it to go?
- What am I tracking that I never look at? Stop tracking it.
- Is my system too heavy to survive a bad week? If yes, simplify it now.
