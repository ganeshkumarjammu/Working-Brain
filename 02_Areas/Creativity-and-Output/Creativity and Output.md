## 🎯 Creative principles
- Volume beats perfection early; quality emerges from quantity
- Ship at 80% — the last 20% is usually for your ego, not the reader
- Create before you consume — mornings for output, evenings for input
- Steal like an artist: combine two influences nobody has combined
- Boredom is a precondition for ideas. Stop filling every gap with a screen.

## 🪞 Review prompts
- What have I made this month that didn't exist before?
- What idea have I been "researching" instead of starting?
- Am I consuming 10x what I create? Fix the ratio.
