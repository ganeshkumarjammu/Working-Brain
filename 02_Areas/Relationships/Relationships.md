# ❤️ Relationships

> The single strongest predictor of a good life. Also the easiest area to let quietly decay while you're "busy".

## 🎯 The standard
- The people who matter hear from me without needing to chase
- I'm present when I'm with people — phone away
- I give more than I take, and I ask when I need help
- I actively make new connections, not just maintain old ones

## 🪞 Review prompts
- Who have I not spoken to in 3+ months that I'd be sad to lose?
- Who has been giving more than receiving in our relationship lately?
- Who drains me, and why do I keep saying yes?
- When did I last reach out to someone with no agenda?
- Am I a person others would want in *their* inner circle?
