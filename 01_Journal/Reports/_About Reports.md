One note per report, each driven by a `period` property. Change the period, switch to Reading view, then tab menu → Export to PDF. Copy any report (or use `T - Report`) to make your own. The catalog of sections you can put in a report is in [[08_Meta/Work-Style/Dataview Cookbook|the Dataview Cookbook]].

| Report | Default period | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [[Weekly Status Report]] | this week | Friday message to your manager |
| [[Monthly Performance Report]] | this month | Trend, leaks, delivery, learning |
| [[Quarterly Evidence Pack]] | this quarter | Reviews and raise conversations |
| [[Timesheet and Attendance Report]] | this month | If your hours are ever questioned |
| [[Time Waste Report]] | last 30 days | Starting a process conversation with facts |
| [[Estimation and Delivery Report]] | last 90 days | Calibrating estimates, task-by-task ledger |
| [[Proof Register]] | last 90 days | Every proof link in one place |
| [[Skills and Learning Report]] | last 90 days | Growth and teaching evidence |
